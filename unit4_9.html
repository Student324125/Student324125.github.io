Chapter I · Lifestyle & Vlogs Sunday Ritual & Reality

A candid rhythm between discipline and emotional reset

Caroline’s audience calls her weekly uploads their Sunday ritual—watched on gym treadmills, over coffee, or during apartment resets. What distinguishes her vlogs from hyper-polished influencer feeds is what she calls the intersection of “real energy and aesthetic”: sharing 16-hour screen-time admissions, coaching reflections on people-pleasing, and quiet breaks from social media alongside luxury press trips.

After stepping back from unsustainable daily Vlogmas marathons to protect her mental bandwidth and personal life, she rebuilt her weekdays around structured “habit stacking,” refined-sugar resets, non-heat hair recovery (sock curls, rosemary-mint scalp oiling, and silk bonnets), and long morning walks with her French bulldog, Sky.

Chapter II · The Matcha Journey Five-Year Full Circle

From self-proclaimed matcha skeptic to every Starbucks in Germany

Five years before her beverage hit menus nationwide, Caroline filmed herself wishing she were “that girl” who genuinely liked matcha—at the time, low-grade blends tasted “fishy” to her. The shift began in New York in 2023, deepened in London when she and her sister Doja tracked down ceremonial-grade whisks and sieves (inspired by Jenki Matcha), and solidified in Kyoto and Osaka tea houses.

When Starbucks Germany invited her to pitch her own creation, she wanted something unexpected yet crowd-pleasing—avoiding predictable mango or strawberry variants. At the very first Berlin tasting, she spontaneously proposed pairing vanilla coconut matcha with an almond cold foam crown. Months later, during a six-course dinner in Cape Town, she received the confirmation text and broke down in tears. Following her nationwide launch, she teased the next step in Tokyo: her own ceremonial line, Senso Matcha.

Chapter III · Brand Architecture N9, Sensu & Collaborations

Built in-house: N9 apparel, Sensu home rituals & angel bets

Unlike typical creator merch lines outsourced to third-party agencies, Caroline and her father operate N9 (Ninety-Nine) entirely in-house. From sourcing raw fabrics and reviewing summer samples to conducting live fittings with her regular size-XL model, Caro personally inspects every garment before sign-off—enforcing a strict rule on extra-long proportions so wearers can shorten hems rather than settle for cropped lengths.

Alongside N9’s mature rebrand and Frankfurt pop-up stores, she expanded her entrepreneurial footprint with Sensu (home fragrance, Palo Santo holders, affirmation cards, and the teased Senso Matcha), co-created beauty hits with Kaja, hosted luxury experiences for Lounge, and made an angel investment in the coconut water startup Avie.

Chapter IV · Style Playbook Summer 2026 & Winter Chic

“Nobody cares—wear what makes you feel good”

Caro’s seasonal style guides deliberately reject rigid fashion labels. One day she leans Parisian or New York editorial; the next, resort-ready or classic corporate. Her wardrobe rule balances investment staples—cashmere knits, silk shirts, and tailored linen—with trend accents that survive more than one season.
