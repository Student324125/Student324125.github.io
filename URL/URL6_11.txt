Final Project: Coconut Smash by Luana 
https://student324125.github.io/CoconutSmash.html
Coconut Smash is a browser-based game where players smash coconuts using real hand gestures in front of their
webcam — no controller needed. It uses MediaPipe HandLandmarker, a machine learning model that tracks hand
movements in real time, running entirely in the browser. The value is twofold: it makes AI tangible and
physically experienceable, while simply being fun to play.
